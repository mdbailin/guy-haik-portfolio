Accessibility

Accessibility statement

Last updated: November 2026

Guy Haik is committed to making this website usable for as many people as possible, including people with disabilities.

What is available

  • An accessibility menu, the round button at the bottom-left of every page. It lets you increase or decrease text size, switch to high contrast, highlight links, use a more readable font, and reset all settings.
  • A responsive layout that works on phones, tablets, and computers.
  • Keyboard access to the accessibility menu. Press Escape to close it.

Our goal

We aim to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA, and the requirements of Israeli Standard 5568. The site has not yet been fully audited, so some parts may not meet these standards.

Known limitations

Some embedded third-party content, such as videos, and some older articles may not be fully accessible.
