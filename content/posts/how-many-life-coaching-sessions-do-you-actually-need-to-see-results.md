When is it time to finish coaching?

There isn't a single moment when everyone should stop.

You might feel ready to finish when you've made progress toward your goals, have a clearer understanding of your situation, or feel more confident handling the challenges that brought you to coaching.

You might also decide to stop because your priorities have changed or the process no longer feels useful. You don't have to reach a particular milestone to be allowed to make that decision.

And finishing doesn't mean you'll never face another difficult decision or need support again. Life continues, circumstances change, and new challenges arise. The point isn't to reach a stage where you never struggle. It's to develop your ability to respond to those challenges with greater understanding and independence.
