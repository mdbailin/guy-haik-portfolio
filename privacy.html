Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

This website (guyhaik.com) is run by Guy Haik. This page explains what personal information is collected when you use it, why, and what choices you have.

What is collected

  • Questions you send. Through the Ask Guy form (hosted on Google Forms) you can send a question, an optional name or nickname, and an optional email address. Please do not include names or details that identify you or other people.
  • Analytics, only if you accept. If you press Accept in the cookie banner, Google Analytics sets cookies that measure visits, pages viewed, approximate location and device type. If you decline, these cookies are not set.
  • Your choices on your device. The site saves your cookie choice and your accessibility settings in your browser, so they are remembered on your next visit.
  • Technical data. Like any website, the hosting provider and the services that load fonts, icons and scripts can see your IP address and browser details when a page loads.

How it is used

  • To read and answer your question. If you leave an email address, it is used only to reply to you.
  • To publish your question on the site, anonymously, only if you tick the box that allows it. Identifying details may be removed or changed before publication.
  • To understand, in aggregate, how the site is used and to improve it.
  • Your information is not sold, and it is not used for marketing emails.

Who else handles it

Google (Google Forms and Google Analytics), GitHub (website hosting), and content providers such as Cloudflare (cdnjs) and jsDelivr, which deliver icons and scripts. If you watch YouTube content, YouTube may also set its own cookies. Some of these providers process data outside Israel, including in the United States.

How long it is kept

Questions are kept for as long as needed to answer them and for reference, and are deleted when you ask. Analytics data is kept according to the retention settings in Google Analytics.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information you sent. You can change your cookie choice at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings, after which the banner appears again.

Sensitive topics

Questions about beliefs, emotions or personal difficulties can be sensitive. Share only what you are comfortable with. This service is not for emergencies. If you are in crisis, please contact local emergency services or a crisis line.

Age

This website is intended for adults.

Contact

For questions or requests about your information, write to guyhaik1811@gmail.com.

Changes
